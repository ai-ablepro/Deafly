Community Guidelines

Welcome! This invite-only group is for Deaf and Hard of Hearing people looking to connect, whether for friendship, dating, or community. All communication styles (ISL, lipreading, text, voice, etc.) are welcome here.

We encourage local meetups ask the admin if you'd like help/support organizing one! Events should be inclusive, accessible, and fun. This was created by your fellow DHH friend, to help make connections more accessible, safe and fun!

Platform rules:
  1. Be kind and respectful no bullying or hate.
  2. All communication styles like ISL, texts, lipreading or speaking are valid. Be respectful of others' communication style.
  3. Ask before messaging someone privately.
  4. No pressure consent is key.
  5. Keep it supportive and safe. Be respectful of others' sexuality/gender, caste, etc.
  6. No spam or promotions. Message an admin for any clarification.
  7. Respect everyone's privacy. This means NO GOSSIP!
  8. Events should be safe & inclusive.
  9. Stay on topic (dating, friendships, D/HoH life)
  10. We do not bear any responsibilities or consequences post your connections through this platform.

We are glad you are here!
